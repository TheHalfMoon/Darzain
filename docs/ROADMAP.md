One experienced builder plus independent review: **24–42 working days** for ten bounded MVP PRs, **5–10 days contingency** for Windows/media/recovery findings and review latency. These are task sums and planning assumptions, not measured speedup or delivery commitments. Re-estimate after DB and preview spikes. Approval/CI waiting, signing procurement and redesign excluded. Desktop adds estimated 8–15 days after ADR; limited AI/Director v1 approximately 8–16 weeks after qualified hardware/accounts/licenses. World leadership has no credible calendar estimate.
